Most homes get their heating, plumbing and ventilation from three different trades. We design all three as one system: central heating, underfloor heating, heat pumps, hot water and balanced ventilation, for homes across Wānaka and Queenstown.

We also service, maintain and repair, including the systems other installers have walked away from.
