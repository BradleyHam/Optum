Servicing · Wānaka & Queenstown

Systems we install, we look after.

Planned servicing and prompt repairs from two local teams, with annual care for heat pumps and boilers plus practical fault-finding when systems stop behaving.

Heating system servicing, boiler servicing, repairs and maintenance across Wānaka and Queenstown, backed by a company operating since 1996.

A well-designed system deserves to be kept at its best. We service heat pumps, boilers and hydronic systems, and diagnose faults when something is no longer working as it should.

What we do

01

Heat-pump servicing Book each autumn

Annual servicing for air-to-water and ground-source heat pumps, helping the system stay efficient, reliable and ready for winter.

02

Gas & diesel boiler servicing

Scheduled boiler servicing and fault diagnosis to keep heating and hot water operating safely, efficiently and reliably.

03

Heating fault-finding & repairs

Careful diagnosis and remedial repairs for heat pumps, boilers and hydronic systems when heat, pressure or controls are not behaving properly.

04

Plumbing & hot-water maintenance

General plumbing repairs plus maintenance of hot-water systems, from leaks and faulty fixtures to inconsistent water temperature.
