Plumbing · Wānaka & Queenstown

Plumbing for the way your home really works.

From new builds and renovations to repairs and off-mains systems, Optum brings plumbing, gas, drainage and hot water together under one experienced team.

Plumbing, gas fitting and drainlaying for new builds, renovations and existing homes across Wānaka and Queenstown. From town supply to off-mains, we make the whole system work.

Optum advises, designs, installs, repairs and maintains complete plumbing systems. Working with homeowners, architects and builders, we plan water supply, hot water, drainage, gas and wastewater around the home and the people who use it.

What we do

01

New builds & renovations Design to handover

Plumbing, gas fitting and drainlaying planned with your architect and builder, then carried through installation, fit-off and handover by one coordinated team.

02

Gas fitting & heating

Certified gas fitting for new installations, upgrades and servicing, including gas hot water, boilers, fires and cooking appliances.

03

Drainlaying & drainage

New drainage systems, stormwater and sewer connections, blockage clearing and repairs to damaged drains for homes and building projects.

04

Hot water systems

Installation, repair and maintenance of cylinders, heat pump hot water and integrated systems, sized around the way your household uses water.

05

Wastewater treatment

Septic and effluent treatment solutions for off-mains properties, including systems that produce treated water suitable for irrigation.

06

Rainwater & water quality

Rainwater tanks, roof-water collection, filtration and treatment for potable household supply, irrigation or other on-site use.

07

Plumbing maintenance & repairs

Help with leaks, faulty fixtures, blocked or burst pipes, repairs and replacements, plus practical advice when an existing system needs attention.
