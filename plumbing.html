Everyday plumbing, done properly. From a dripping tap to a new bathroom, one local team looks after it.

Optum’s plumbers, gasfitters and drainlayers work across Wānaka, Queenstown and Cromwell. We fix leaks and blocked drains, replace hot water cylinders, plumb renovations and new builds, and give you a clear explanation and a clear quote before the work starts.

What we can help with

01

Leaks, repairs & maintenance

Leaking pipes, dripping taps, running toilets, low water pressure and faulty fixtures. We find the cause, fix it properly and leave the place tidy.

02

Hot water cylinders

No hot water, a leaking cylinder or rising power bills. We repair and replace electric, gas and heat pump hot water systems, sized to how your household actually uses water.

03

Bathroom, kitchen & laundry renovations

Moving a shower, adding a vanity or starting from scratch. We plan the pipework with you or your builder, then handle rough-in, fit-off and sign-off.

04

Blocked drains & drainlaying

Blocked sinks, toilets and outside drains cleared, damaged drains repaired, and new stormwater and sewer connections laid by certified drainlayers.

05

Gas fitting

Certified gas fitting for hobs, fires, gas hot water and boilers, including new connections, swaps and safety checks.

06

New builds & extensions

Plumbing, gas and drainage for new homes and extensions, coordinated with your architect and builder from plans through to handover.

07

Rainwater, water treatment & wastewater

Rainwater tanks, filtration and UV treatment, plus septic and wastewater systems for rural and off-mains properties.

Common questions

Before you get in touch.

Still not sure? Call 0800 467 886 and we’ll talk it through.

Do you take on small plumbing jobs?

Yes. Dripping taps, leaking pipes, running toilets, blocked drains and faulty fixtures are everyday work for our team, alongside larger renovations and new builds.

Which areas do you cover?

We’re based in Wānaka and work across Queenstown and the wider Queenstown Lakes district, including Cromwell and Central Otago.

Can you replace my hot water cylinder?

Yes. We repair and replace electric, gas and heat pump hot water cylinders, and can talk you through whether a like-for-like swap or a more efficient system makes more sense for your household.

Do you handle emergency plumbing?

Yes. For burst pipes, major leaks and other urgent plumbing problems, call us directly on 0800 467 886 rather than using the online form.

Can you help with a bathroom renovation?

Yes. We plumb bathroom, kitchen and laundry renovations, from moving fixtures and replacing pipework to fit-off, and work alongside your builder or designer if you have one.
