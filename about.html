About

Locally owned, and quietly obsessed with getting it right.

Optum has been designing and installing heating, plumbing and energy systems since 1996, and today serves Wānaka and Queenstown.

One plumber became two teams of plumbers, gasfitters, heating specialists and solar installers. We installed Wānaka's first solar hot water system in 2010, and have worked alongside architects since long before that was common here.

The people who turn up at your home tend to be the same people, year after year. Our clients ask for them by name.

How we work

01 Design before installation
01

Design-led, not install-only

We work with architects from the design phase, engineering the system into the home rather than bolting it on afterwards. Better Living by Design is a method, not a slogan.

02

One integrated system

Heating, plumbing, ventilation, hot water and energy are designed to work together. Most companies do one piece; we design the whole climate and water story of a house.

03

Whole-of-life thinking

We design for the decades you will live with the system, not the week it takes to install. Comfort, health and low running costs over the long run beat the cheapest quote every time.

04

Local, and here to stay

We know the alpine climate, the building stock and the architects, because we have worked beside them for nearly thirty years. When you need us again, we will still be here.
