Heating · Wānaka & Queenstown

Warmth, engineered into the fabric.

Whole-home heating that holds an even, gentle warmth through the coldest alpine winters. Efficient, silent, and invisible in daily life.

Central heating, hydronic underfloor heating and heat pumps for homes across Wānaka and Queenstown, sized, zoned and designed from the first drawings.

Heating is our signature work. We calculate what your home actually needs, then design one system where the heat source, underfloor heating, hot water and controls work together. It runs quietly, costs little and simply disappears into daily life.

What we do

01

Central heating Signature work

Whole-home hydronic heating designed around how you live: one heat source feeding underfloor heating and hot water, zoned and controlled around the needs of the home.

02

Hydronic underfloor heating

Warm water pipework in the slab, delivering an even, silent warmth across the whole floor. The most comfortable way to heat an alpine home, and among the cheapest to run.

03

Air-to-water & ground source heat pumps

Renewable heat sources sized to your home's real demand. As a preferred Stiebel Eltron installer, we design, install and service systems built for Central Otago winters.

04

Hot water systems

Hot water heat pumps, cylinders and boiler systems that deliver constant hot water without constant costs, integrated with the heating rather than running beside it.

05

Solar hot water & solar energy

Optum has been designing and installing solar hot water systems in Wānaka since at least 2010. Solar thermal and PV are planned to work with your heat pump and storage, not bolted alongside them.
