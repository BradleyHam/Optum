# Optum — Business & Brand Context

Reference file for anyone designing or writing for the Optum website. Everything here is drawn from their existing site, Google profile, customer reviews and market research.

---

## The one-line summary

Optum designs, installs and maintains premium, integrated heating, plumbing and energy systems for high-end architectural homes across Wānaka and the Queenstown Lakes district.

**Tagline:** Better Living by Design.

## Positioning — what makes them different

This is the heart of the brand and it should come through everywhere.

**1. Design-led, not install-only.** They work with architects from the design phase, engineering the system into the home rather than bolting it on afterwards. "Better Living by Design" is literal, not a slogan.

**2. Integrated, interconnected systems.** The differentiator is that heating, plumbing, ventilation, hot water and energy are designed to work together as one system. Most competitors do one piece. Optum designs the whole climate and water story of a house.

**3. Premium, whole-of-life thinking.** They sell comfort, health and low running costs over decades, not the cheapest install. Warm, dry, healthy homes with minimal power bills.

**4. Genuine expertise and depth.** 28 years, international training, dedicated heating specialists, early adopters of solar thermal and heat pump technology. They understand systems others can't diagnose.

**5. Local and trusted.** Wānaka and Queenstown Lakes specialists. They know the alpine climate, the building stock and the architects.

## Who the customer is

- Building or substantially renovating a **high-end home** in Wānaka or Queenstown, often architecturally designed, often a new build or a passive/high-performance house.
- Frequently working **with an architect or builder**, sometimes referred by them.
- Values **comfort, health, quiet, and low running costs** over the cheapest quote.
- Fed up with **cold, draughty homes and rising power bills** — this is the emotional trigger.
- Also: property managers, and owners of existing systems needing servicing or a replacement.

They are not chasing 2am burst-pipe callouts. Their customer is planning something, not panicking.

## Tone of voice

- Confident, understated, design-led. A premium studio that lets the work speak.
- Warm and human, not corporate. Real people, real homes.
- Plain, specific language over jargon and hype.
- Short sentences. No exclamation marks. No "NZ's number 1", no "we're passionate about", no stock trade clichés.
- Technical when it earns trust (system types, efficiency, design process), never showing off.

**Words that fit:** designed, integrated, considered, comfort, warmth, healthy, efficient, quiet, whole-home, performance, craft.
**Words to avoid:** cheap, fast, deals, hustle, "your local plumbing heroes", anything jokey.

## Visual direction

- **Feeling:** alpine luxury. The contrast between a cold, dramatic Southern Alps landscape outside and a warm, calm, beautifully designed home inside. Warmth engineered into a home.
- **Reference point:** a high-end architecture or interiors studio site, not a typical trades site.
- **Palette (updated):** the brand is moving away from the old deep green. The new direction is a deep navy blue as the primary brand colour, a warm off-white/bone for light sections, and a single restrained warm bronze accent. Warm neutrals throughout, never cool grey.
  - Deep navy (primary): `#003352`, deepening to `#00263E` for footers and darker bands
  - Bone / warm off-white (light sections): `#F6F2EA`, with a lighter paper tone `#FBF9F4`
  - Warm bronze (accent — eyebrows, italic emphasis, primary buttons): `#A9814E`, softer tint `#C19A6B`
  - Near-black ink (body text): `#0E1417`
  - Use bronze sparingly: small letter-spaced labels, the italic word in a headline, and the primary CTA. It should feel like an accent, not a second brand colour.
- **Type:** elegant editorial serif for headlines, clean humanist sans for body. Generous sizing and line height.
- **Imagery:** large, warm architectural photography. Modern alpine interiors, living spaces, the inside/outside contrast, real installed systems. Avoid stock tradespeople, thumbs-ups, clip-art icons.
- **Layout:** generous whitespace, restraint, hairline dividers, small letter-spaced labels. Space is the luxury signal.

## Website priorities (context for structure and copy)

- **Lead with heating.** It's their most valuable, most differentiated and most winnable work. Plumbing supports it.
- **Simple navigation, real depth underneath.** A clean 8-item menu, with a dedicated page per specific service beneath it (underfloor heating, central heating, heat pumps, radiators, hot water, gas fitting, drain laying, etc.) rather than one lumped page per category.
- **Serve both towns.** Homepage speaks to Wānaka and Queenstown Lakes; individual service pages target one town each.
- **Show the work.** Project pages, one per completed job, with real photos and the actual system installed.
- **Keep it uncluttered.** The current site repeats the same testimonials on nearly every page and buries the actual service content. Avoid that.
